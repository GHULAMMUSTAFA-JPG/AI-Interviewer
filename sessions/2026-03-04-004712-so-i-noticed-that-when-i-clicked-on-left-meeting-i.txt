● Found it. There are two issues causing the delay:

  1. _watch_for_leave polls every 5 seconds — but that alone isn't 3-4 minutes
  2. The Leave button click may fail silently — if the Google Meet aria-label
  doesn't match any of the 3 selectors, _watch_for_leave returns without the
  browser actually leaving the meeting. The Python tasks are cancelled, but
  Chrome stays connected to the Meet session until the ctx.close() eventually
  resolves or Chrome times out on its own (hence the 3-4 minute window)

  The fix: reduce polling to 1 second, and add a guaranteed
  page.goto("about:blank") fallback after the button click attempt — this
  navigates Chrome away from Meet regardless of whether the button click worked.

● Two changes made to bot.py:

  1. Polling interval: 5s → 1s — the watcher now reacts within ~1 second of the
  status change instead of up to 5 seconds.
  2. page.goto("about:blank") fallback — after attempting the Leave button
  click, Chrome is forcibly navigated away from the Meet URL. This is the key
  fix: even if all 3 button selectors fail (e.g. Google Meet updated its DOM and
   the aria-labels changed), the browser disconnects from the meeting
  immediately rather than staying connected until Chrome eventually times out
  3-4 minutes later.
