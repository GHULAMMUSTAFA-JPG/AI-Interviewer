  Plan 2: Interruption — Plain English Explanation

  What happens right now (the problem):

  The AI bot is talking (audio playing in the meeting). The candidate starts
  talking over it — they interrupt. But the bot finishes its entire sentence
  before it stops. Feels rude and robotic.

  What we want:

  The moment the candidate says a word — bot stops talking immediately.

  How it works technically (3 steps):

  1. Candidate speaks
     ↓
     Meeting-Bot already detects this (it reads Google Meet captions)

  2. Meeting-Bot writes one flag to MongoDB:
     interviews → {tts_interrupt: true}

  3. TTS container is watching MongoDB.
     It sees that flag → immediately stops audio playback

  That's it. No new services. MongoDB is already running and both containers
  already connect to it. We just use it as a simple signal.

  The audio player in TTS already supports stopping mid-stream — we just need to
   trigger it from MongoDB instead of from NATS (which we don't have running).
