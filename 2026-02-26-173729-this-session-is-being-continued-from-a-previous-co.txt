  Bug 1 — Mic muted in Google Meet:
  Added unmute check in bot.py after joining. After popup dismissal, it looks
  for button[aria-label="Unmute microphone"] and clicks it. Also logs whether it
   unmuted or was already active — visible in VNC.

  Bug 2 — Audio cut off at 3 seconds:
  audio_player.py timeout was hardcoded to 3s. Now it calculates
  expected_drain_sec = bytes_written / (22050 * 2) + 5s buffer. A 504 KB
  response (11.4s audio) now gets a 16.4s drain window instead of being killed
  at 3s.
